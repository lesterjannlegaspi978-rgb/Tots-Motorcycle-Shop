Fast & Slow Moving Products

Choose a time period to see how often each product was sold.

Fast Moving: the product has sales every day within the selected period, even if only one piece a day.
Slow Moving: the product has sales, but on and off, with some days that have sales and some days that have none.
No Sales: the product has no sales at all within the selected period.
